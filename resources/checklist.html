Checklist

Idea to published — one page.

Print it, stick it on the wall, tick the boxes.

1. Idea

  • Game in one sentence
  • One main action
  • How you win / lose
  • 3 things NOT to add yet

2. Tools

  • Picked one tool (Start here page)
  • Installed or opened it
  • Finished its first tutorial

3. Build

  • Player moves / acts
  • Something to avoid or solve
  • Score or goal shows on screen
  • Win and game-over screens
  • Play again button

4. Polish

  • Free art or your own art
  • Sounds for main actions
  • Credits file for assets
  • Someone else playtested it
  • Fixed the top 3 problems

5. Share

  • Exported a build
  • Cover image + screenshots
  • Published (itch.io or web)
  • Told one person
