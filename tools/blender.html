Who it’s for

Best for teens and adults, and for people who have made something before.

What it costs

Free. Free to use. Check the official site for any limits.

Good stuff

  • Costs nothing to use
  • Open source — free forever, community-run
  • Runs on Windows, Mac, Linux

Watch out for

  • Not meant for young kids
  • Age rules apply — see below

Your first steps

  1. Install it from the official site.
  2. Follow the official beginner guide for 30 minutes.
  3. Make one small asset at the size your game needs.
  4. Export it (PNG for 2D, glTF for 3D) and drop it into your engine.
