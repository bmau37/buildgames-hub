Who it’s for

Good stuff

Your first steps

  1. Download it from the official site (link above).
  2. Open the official “first project” tutorial or a starter template.
  3. Make a tiny game with one action — move and jump, or click and score.
  4. Export it to the web or your computer and let someone play.
