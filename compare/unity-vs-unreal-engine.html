Quick take

Choose Unity if its ecosystem fits your project; choose Unreal Engine if its ecosystem fits your project. Beginners should favour the tool marked 🟢. Always check the official sites for current pricing.

💡 Unity: Unity Personal is free if revenue + funding ≤ US$200K (prior 12 months); Unity Pro is required above that (US$2,310/seat/yr). The Runtime Fee was cancelled in Sept 2024; no royalties.

💡 Unreal Engine: Epic Games Store revenue is royalty-exempt. Non-game commercial users with >US$1M annual revenue need seats at US$1,850/yr.
