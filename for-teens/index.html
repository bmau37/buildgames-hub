Level up from blocks to real engines.

51 tools that fit teens — picked for difficulty and each tool's official age rules.

Your first steps

  1. Pick Godot (free) or GameMaker for 2D.
  2. Follow one free course start-to-finish (GDQuest or CS50).
  3. Join a game jam — 48 hours, one small game.
  4. Upload to itch.io with a parent's OK (publishing is 18+ or with guardian consent).
