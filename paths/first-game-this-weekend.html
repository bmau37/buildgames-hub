Make your first game this weekend

🟢 Beginner
  1. Pick a tiny idea — One mechanic, one screen. Think Pong, Flappy, or a 3-room text adventure.
    Twine Bitsy
  2. Choose a friendly tool — Godot or GDevelop for 2D; PICO-8 if you love limits.
    Godot Engine GDevelop PICO-8
  3. Grab free assets — Use CC0 art and generated sound effects so you can focus on play.
    Kenney Assets Bfxr / jsfxr
  4. Ask AI when stuck — Paste errors into an assistant and ask it to explain, not just fix.
    Claude ChatGPT
  5. Ship it — Upload a web build to itch.io (free) and share the link.
    itch.io
